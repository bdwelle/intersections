Intersections is a writing and visual art project documenting stories of people in San Francisco connected to specific street intersections. 

* SF residents will submit stories associated with a specific street intersection
* I will select stories that sound most interesting 
* I will interview the subjects and collect audio of the interview and photos of the subject and the intersection, being sure to include the street signs of the two intersecting streets themselves. 
* photos will be processed to produce images for Instagram and t-shirts 
* each subject gets their own custom t-shirt and the shirt also becomes available for sale (made by posting as a product on teepublic) 
* audio gets parsed and used with imagery to create a “story” post. 
* all posts are available in a list but also on a clickable map of the city. 

system requirements

1. web site built using modern tools, to be hosted on a self-hosting platform like Dreamhost, easily editable and maintainable by humans with good technical skills (like me). 
2. story submission function 
    1. select an intersection - search by street names or visually, by clicking on a map. this will require a full database of streets and intersections for the search function 
    2. submit name, email, phone, history of living in SF, story synopsis
3. submission review and selection 
    1. view submissions, add notes, status, rating 
    2. columns should be sortable 
4. interview handling 
    1. add audio, photos, full story text, summary, etc
5. publish interview
    1. publish a post which becomes available as as discrete web page, in a list, and on a clickable map of the city 

The visual design should be kind of blown out street photography with fish eye, wide angle lens, high contrast, black and white, where the street signs are kind of blown up like helicopter blades and they're they're lit by flash or the glow of street lights at night through the fog, or evening light at the golden hour, an hour before sunset. 

Propose an open-source system architecture, specifically addressing each of the requirements and how you would build that.
